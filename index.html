FABLE

A desktop application for planning, researching and writing video productions. Created by Mr T.

What it is

FABLE runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It keeps its projects, scripts and research in a folder on that computer, and it is operated by the one person who installed it. It is a personal tool: it is not offered as a service, and there is nothing to sign up for.

How it uses a YouTube account

The person who runs FABLE can connect their own YouTube channel to it, by signing in with Google in their own browser. FABLE asks for permission to read: the channel's name, its public counters (subscribers, views, videos) and totals from the channel owner's YouTube Analytics. For other channels that person names, it reads the same public counters, to compare. It asks for nothing that can change, upload, publish or delete anything.

What it reads is shown to that person on their own screen and kept on their own computer. It is not sent to the maker of FABLE or to anyone else. The privacy policy says this in full.

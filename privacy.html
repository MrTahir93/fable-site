FABLE — Privacy policy

Last changed on 18 November 2026.

Who this is about

FABLE is a desktop application that runs on the computer of the person who installed it. That person is its only user. FABLE has no server, no user accounts and no shared database: there is no place outside that computer where FABLE keeps anything.

What FABLE reads from Google

When its user connects their own YouTube channel, by signing in with Google in their own browser, FABLE receives permission to read, and reads:

  • the channel's name, handle and channel ID;
  • the channel's public counters: subscribers, views and number of videos;
  • totals from the channel owner's YouTube Analytics for a run of days: views, minutes watched, average view duration, subscribers gained and subscribers lost;
  • for other YouTube channels that the user names, so as to compare with them: the name, handle and channel ID of each, and its public counters — subscribers, views and number of videos. These are figures anyone can see on those channels; nothing private of another channel is read, and nothing of it can be.

FABLE asks only for permissions that read (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly). It cannot change the channel, upload, publish, comment or delete. It does not read e-mail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How it is used

What is read is shown to the user on their own screen, in the application, so that they can see how their channel is doing. It is used for nothing else. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not given to any AI model.

Where it is kept

The figures are kept in FABLE's database, in a folder on the user's own computer. The sign-in itself (an authorization token) is kept encrypted by Windows for the signed-in Windows user, and in no file of that folder. A figure is never written into the application's activity log.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. FABLE sends what it reads from Google to no one: not to its maker, and not to any other person, company or service.

Keeping and deleting

The figures stay on the user's computer until the user deletes them, by deleting FABLE's data folder. The maker of FABLE holds no copy and has nothing to delete.

Taking the access away

The user can take FABLE's access to their channel away at any time: with the Disconnect button in FABLE, which takes the authorization back at Google and deletes it from the computer, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google's rules

FABLE's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If FABLE comes to read anything else, or to use what it reads in another way, this page is changed first, and its date with it.
